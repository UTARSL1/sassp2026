§ 01

Overview

The IEEE Signal Processing Society Malaysia Chapter and Universiti Tunku Abdul Rahman jointly organise the 14ᵗʰ Symposium on Acoustics, Speech and Signal Processing on 10 August 2026 — a free, in-person gathering open to students, academics, industry professionals and IEEE / non-IEEE members alike.

Objectives
  • Bring the university and industry together to share and discuss the latest trends in signal processing.
  • Serve as a forum for knowledge sharing on signal processing technology and for professional networking.
